Travel and Expense Policy

Policy FIN-007, maintained by Finance (finance@acme.example). Applies to all employees and contractors.

Booking Travel

All flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel platform (TravelPerk) at least 14 days before departure when possible. Bookings made later require a short justification.

  • Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
  • Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.
  • Business class requires approval from a VP.

Daily Limits

CategoryLimit
Hotel (per night, Europe)180 EUR

Submitting Expenses

Submit expenses in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase. Every expense above 25 EUR needs an itemized receipt. Expenses submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll after manager approval.

Non-reimbursable Items

  • Alcohol, except during approved client entertainment.
  • Traffic and parking fines.
  • Upgrades purchased without approval, minibar and in-room movies.
