# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Version 4.2 — effective January 1, 2026. Owner: People Operations (people@acme.example).

## 1. Working Hours

Standard working hours are 09:00–18:00, Monday to Friday, including a one hour lunch break.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00; meetings should be scheduled inside core hours whenever possible.
Flexible start times between 07:30 and 10:00 are allowed with manager approval.

## 2. Leave Policy

### 2.1 Annual Leave

- Full-time employees receive **20 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- After 5 years of continuous service the entitlement increases to **25 working days**.
- Up to **5 unused days** can be carried over to the next year and must be used before March 31.
- Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal (Workday) at least **10 working days** in advance for
  absences longer than 3 days.

### 2.2 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences
longer than 3 consecutive days. Notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

### 2.3 Parental Leave

- Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
- Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
- Parental leave can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## 3. Compensation & Benefits

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Salary review | Annually in April, based on performance review results |
| Health insurance | Private plan covering employee, spouse and children; 100% premium paid by Acme |
| Meal allowance | 400 EUR per month loaded on the meal card |
| Learning budget | 1,500 EUR per year for courses, books and conferences |
| Home office stipend | One-time 600 EUR for new hires, plus 30 EUR monthly internet allowance |

## 4. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in March and September. Each review includes a self assessment,
peer feedback from at least two colleagues and a one-to-one with the manager. Ratings use a 5-point scale:
Exceptional, Exceeds, Meets, Partially Meets, Does Not Meet.

## 5. Code of Conduct

Acme has zero tolerance for harassment and discrimination. Concerns can be reported to your manager,
People Operations, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at ethics.acme.example. Retaliation against anyone
who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
